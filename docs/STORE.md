## Full description

Pair Buddy is a private space for you and your partner. Answer a daily
question together, keep your photos in one shared album, draw each other
little pictures, and see how far apart you are when distance gets in the way.

What is inside:

- Daily Spark: one thoughtful question every day. Answer it, guess your
  partner's answer, and compare.
- Quiz packs: playful question sets about your memories, dreams, and daily
  life.
- Memories: a shared photo album only the two of you can see.
- Drawing canvas: sketch something and send it straight to your partner.
- Live distance: share your location with each other and see the distance
  between you, in kilometers or miles.
- Date planner: browse date ideas, save plans, and let the AI date planner
  suggest something new.
- Discussion: a private chat thread for the two of you.
- Home screen widgets: your countdown, daily spark, or distance, right on
  your home screen.

Everything is free. Your photos and messages stay between you and your
partner.

## Privacy notes (for the data safety / privacy sections)

- Location: optional, shared only with your paired partner, only while the
  sharing toggle is on. Turning it off deletes your stored position.
- Photos: stored in private cloud storage, visible only to the two paired
  accounts.
- Contacts: the app does not read your contacts. Pairing works through an
  invite code you share yourself.
- Account deletion: deleting your account removes your profile, photos,
  messages, and location data.
- No advertising SDKs, no data sold, no third-party analytics.
